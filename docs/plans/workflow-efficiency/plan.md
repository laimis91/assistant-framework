## Implementation decisions

User decisions recorded on 2026-10-27 during implementation review; they override conflicting proposals below.

- **D1 — no-change REFACTOR reuse.** GREEN is reused when there were no edits after GREEN and the caller-resolved current `reuse_digest` equals `green.reuse_digest` (default `reuse_identity_mode=digest`). Both digests are defined solely by assistant-tdd `contracts/output.yaml` `green.reuse_digest`. The seven-category `relevant_input_identity` plus `verification_reuse_authority` apply only to persisted packets that opt into `detailed` mode. Failed GREEN is never reused; zero new invocations and the exact original GREEN run set remain required.
- **D2 — light review.** Trivial/small light work uses one newly created fresh-context reviewer covering spec and quality, plus one fresh re-review after a fix. Medium+ keeps at least two sibling-blind passes. This supersedes "keep the current number of passes" below for light work only.
- **U1 — one acceptance owner (2026-10-28).** The final fresh acceptance review (`review_subject=acceptance`) is the single owner of request, plan and delivery reconciliation. Stage 1 Spec Review is no longer a separate agent; its checks (unfinished request/plan items, missing acceptance criteria, extra scope, changed-files mismatch, verification mismatch) are spec obligations of the final review batch, recorded in `spec_review_result`. Plan review uses `review_subject=plan`. Medium minimum is the plan reviewer plus a final batch at the canonical per-size count (medium 3) of sibling-blind passes. Historical Stage 1 records stay readable. This supersedes "keep the current number of passes" below for the Stage 1 agent only.
- **U2 — compact light review (2026-10-28).** Trivial/small light review records a compact result: fresh reviewer identity (actor id, native invocation id, `fork_turns=none` on Codex), PASS or findings, evidence refs and reviewed scope. It needs no batch plan, ledgers, structured `fresh_dispatch` or review-tools validation unless persisted/executable admission, an Architecture Decision Pack or expanded impact requires the structured path. Workflow passes its triage `controller_intensity` to Review; a one-pass light topology requires `controller_intensity=light` and `topology.light_review=true`. One fresh re-review after a fix still applies.
- **U3 — caller-trust boundary (2026-10-28).** The concrete admission bypasses found in review (B1–B7) are fixed. Caller-resolved values such as attestation, current digests and actor ids are trusted inputs; independence is guaranteed by fresh reviewers, not by the admission script. No hardening beyond those listed items is added.
- **U4 — one-pass plan review (2026-10-28).** A batch with `review_subject=plan` uses exactly one fresh discovery pass with perspective `plan_coverage_and_feasibility` (request coverage, resolved decisions, feasibility, dependency order, verification oracles, completion allocation, avoidable work) and `topology.plan_review=true`. After a material plan fix, one newly created fresh re-review of the same perspective receives the closure ledger. Code/acceptance review keeps its standard topology. Medium minimum becomes one plan reviewer plus the final batch; admission still consumes one plan-review authority. Historical multi-pass plan reviews stay readable; new plan reviews use exactly one pass. This refines U1's plan-review count.
- **U5 — compact plan review (2026-10-28).** Plan review (`review_subject=plan`) records a compact result, the same condition light review uses: one newly created fresh reviewer, `reviewer_identity`, PASS or findings rows `{aggregate_finding_id, file_line, description}`, request/plan coverage reusing the `request_plan_reconciliation` field names (required/observed/unresolved requirement ids and unresolved finding ids), and `closure_results` from one newly created fresh re-reviewer after a material plan fix. When persisted/executable admission applies, the structured plan batch and review-tools validation are used unchanged. This refines U4's record.
- **WE-07 tooling deferral.** Snapshot, manifest and byte-copy tooling is deferred by the user; that part of WE-07 is not delivered by this implementation.
- **Historical review records.** [review-record.json](review-record.json) and [source-manifest.json](source-manifest.json) are preparation-time records of plan digest `8c8879df56922dbaa791bec4b1bf61a9332c016ee72c3707bf9fdf8beafa2075` (2026-10-26); they do not cover these Implementation decisions or the implementation.
- **Supporting installer change.** `install.sh` and `install.ps1` exclude `__pycache__` and `*.pyc` from copied tools, and `install.ps1` matches wildcard exclusions, because Python checks create caches under `tools/`.

The inspected source baseline is `c920fd89ee232032aafd2541dd51c9c5bd9744f3`, plus the pre-existing dirty state recorded in [source-manifest.json](source-manifest.json). Installed files contain later lifecycle/MCP guidance; the review-efficiency worktree also has overlapping uncommitted changes. Resolve both before implementation; this plan does not designate the old checkout as the release baseline.

## Intended behavior

Codex chooses whether to delegate implementation, mapping, architecture, testing and other execution work. Framework size/risk labels do not force worker agents or prohibit the main agent from implementing. Responsibilities, accepted outcomes, permissions, meaningful TDD when selected, and honest verification remain controlling regardless of who executes.

Two kinds of work retain mandatory isolation: selected research lenses run as separate sibling-blind agents; reviews run in new agents with fresh context. Single-agent role-play, an implementer's self-check, and a previously used reviewer do not establish those independent judgments.

Verification stops when current evidence covers the accepted claims. An unchanged no-refactor assessment can reuse GREEN without another invocation. Completion still requires all accepted work and material review findings to be resolved or explicitly reported unfinished.

## Decisions proposed for approval

### Delegation and independent judgment

Remove obligatory Mapper/Explorer/Architect/writer/Builder-Tester/QA spawn chains and the rule that an orchestrator cannot edit source. Keep specialist roles as optional capabilities. A specialist may still be needed for expertise; that does not automatically require a separate process.

Keep research-lens isolation and all formal review isolation. Current Research v4 permits evidenced sequential fallback for lenses and peer review; the proposed rule deliberately changes that policy. New five-lens work cannot be completed through either fallback path: unavailable isolation remains incomplete. A later explicit user opt-out may change the requested method to ordinary source research, with the changed scope recorded; it cannot certify sequential work as an independently completed five-lens run. Architecture challenge or QA acceptance work that requires independent judgment is performed as a fresh review, without prescribing an additional execution-only agent. Review/QA responsibilities remain distinct even when their dispatch policy is simplified. Thinking perspectives outside a selected research-lens or review method become discretionary; do not describe sequential role-play as independent evidence.

Current light work permits a self-review completion path. Replace that substitute with a fresh reviewer wherever a formal completion review is due; a local self-check remains useful preparation. Plan review applies to an actual requested/required plan and is not recursive: do not create a plan solely to review the act of drafting a plan.

Keep the current number/perspectives of independent review passes for this change. Removing mandatory review-pass counts is not part of this plan; fewer non-review workers must not silently narrow review coverage.

### Verification reuse

Remove the unconditional distinct REFACTOR invocation from TDD prose and executable gates. Keep the refactor assessment itself, including `none needed`.

Extend the existing TDD phase evidence with an explicit reuse disposition and reference to the original GREEN invocation. For current unmodified inputs, REFACTOR may reference the same invocation; a reused record is not a newly executed result or another test count. Bind reuse to relevant inputs, not unrelated document changes. Unknown or mutable inputs require an appropriate freshness check or rerun. Binding fresh-run mandates retain their exact source.

Do not add an extra re-review merely because verification was reused. Any actual review fix still invalidates the affected review evidence, receives due checks and returns to fresh review. Do not remove the post-fix review that protects against unresolved findings.

### Evidence handling

Reuse the existing review result identities, response events, finding ledger, canonical work plan and verification assessment. Remove duplicate narratives/serialized projections where no consumer needs them. Keep sufficient actual commands/results and scope identity for a reviewer to check the claims.

The active review-efficiency worktree already contains `skills/assistant-review/scripts/review-tools.py`, retained-result deduplication and related tests. Reconcile or integrate that work through its owner before extending it; do not create a competing validator, vendor another parser or copy its uncommitted files blindly. The plan can proceed to source implementation after the integration baseline is resolved, but completion of those benefits depends on the actual integrated helper.

Create necessary review material from a frozen read-only source or direct file references. If copying bytes is required, preserve them byte-for-byte and validate the copy mechanically before dispatch. Eliminate model-authored hash tables and fixture rewriting during evidence preparation. A valid schema or hash is structural evidence, never proof that review occurred or a finding was resolved.

Keep the detail of progress/admission/history protocols at their actual persisted/executable boundaries. The common guidance used by every activity should state the outcome and guard briefly and link to wire detail only when a consumer needs it. Retain those rules at the linked boundary; do not remove enforcement while reducing routine loading.

### Plan review and completion

Use the existing review loop and finding/closure identities for plan artifacts. A fresh reviewer checks request coverage, resolved decisions, feasibility, dependency order, verification oracles, completion allocation and avoidable work. Correct material findings in the plan, validate the changed document, and obtain fresh review. Approval does not substitute for review, and review does not grant implementation authority.

At final delivery, the existing independent review checks the original requirement IDs and approved plan against actual results, including deferred/task-wide work. Reuse existing acceptance and completion allocation; do not add a second plan-progress ledger or a separate mandatory completion agent. Mark blocked, failed and unimplemented items explicitly. A partial result is a valid handoff, not completed delivery. Material scope changes require the existing user decision.

## Compatibility design

This changes required protocol constraints, so instructions alone are insufficient. Use versioned producer/consumer changes: Workflow v15 → v16, TDD v2 → v3, Review v9 → v10, Research v4 → v5, and Debugging v2 → v3 where its required TDD result changes. Preserve Verification v1 when its existing evidence assessment can represent reuse without weakening required fields; bump it only if a required serialized constraint actually changes.

Research v5 requires delegated separate lenses and a new fresh peer reviewer for current five-lens completion. Retain read-only interpretation of historical v4 `sequential_fallback`, its reduced-independence label and original policy/provenance. Preserve the two existing positive fallback fixtures as explicitly historical v4 controls; add current-v5 rejection coverage for lens fallback, peer fallback and reuse/relabeling of a v4 fallback record as v5 completion. Migrate the owning semantic validator, contracts, fixtures and actual consumers together. A fully delegated v4 record can be reused only after independently checking the required actor/context/source identity against current obligations; missing facts stay unknown and do not authorize an invented v5 run. Do not destroy valid historical evidence or silently change its old verdict.

Keep old persisted records readable with their original labels and actual failures/runs. A completed historical distinct REFACTOR run remains valid evidence. A partial old log cannot be retrospectively certified: the owner must establish present input identity and an explicit supported reuse decision before current completion. Do not invent historical commands, actor IDs, RED or independent review.

The direct actor is the actual main execution identity, not a pretend native child. Keep worker-stage/actor ownership requirements for genuine delegated work. Map legacy lanes only from retained source/actor evidence; reject contradictions and leave missing required identity as an honest reconciliation gap. Preserve approval identity and shared guard history; representation migration does not reset recovery or authorize scope changes.

Selective installation must not pair new producers with incompatible retained consumers silently. Update the dependency/update checks and installer fixtures using the existing installer mechanism; report incompatible retained versions rather than silently weakening their gates. New plan-review requirements apply to newly authored or materially revised plans; already-running approved plans retain their authority and do not restart solely because the framework was upgraded.
