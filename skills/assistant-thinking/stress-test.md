# Stress Test

Challenge a proposal by identifying the claims that its success depends on. Use evidence, logic, boundary cases, alternatives, downstream effects, and practical constraints where relevant; there is no required number of claims or attack angles.

State the strongest defensible version of the proposal and the strongest evidence-backed rebuttal. Distinguish demonstrated flaws from uncertain risks. Recommend proceeding, revising, or abandoning it with the decisive reason, remaining uncertainty, and a useful validation step.

For requested independent or high-stakes analysis, follow the [independence and delegation rule](SKILL.md#independence-and-delegation). Independent roles receive the same decision facts and scoped angles. Integrated calls and requested structured output use the [canonical contracts](SKILL.md#integrated-and-structured-output). For a small decision without requested independent work, a concise local challenge is enough.
