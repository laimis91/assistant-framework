# Creative Options

Use for naming or approaches where obvious choices feel stale. Generate varied directions, including an unconventional option where useful, then compare them against the user's constraints. If ideas are near-copies, try inversion or a different analogy instead of padding the list.

Show the strongest options with a brief rationale and recommendation. Do not invent sampling probabilities, quality gains, or precision unsupported by an observed evaluation. Use `assistant-ideate` when the task needs broader option generation and ranking.
